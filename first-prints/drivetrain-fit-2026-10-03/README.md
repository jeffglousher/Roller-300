# Unpowered one-drive fit set

Use PETG and the X2D0.4 mm nozzle. Meshes are native CAD exports with rigid bed placement only. `source.json` identifies source hash, body IDs, quantities and bounds; `slice-checks.json` records offline vendor slices. No print was sent.

Start with reaction plate, **two pressure pads**, shaft-clamp trial, input-pulley clamp trial and retainers to check nuts, heads and clamps. Then print carrier and roof-open section. Section approximately187 g /5.2 hours; carrier55 g /2.2 hours on the recorded0.2 mm /5-wall /15% gyroid PETG profile.

Print **two copies of `clutch-pressure-pad.stl`** per drive. The other12 manifest entries require one each. The closed ring under `superseded` cannot assemble around the integral pulley flanges; do not use it.

Follow `../../PLAN.md` for hardware and assembly order. Load fixed-frame nuts before carrier installation; outer nut uses its side port. Check bearing rotation after tightening, full collar engagement, measured endplay, carrier travel and driver access. The section has its upper guard removed; this evidence does not release the complete barrel.

The servo shaft clamp is a trial. Final horn holes, boss clearance, engagement and axial stack need actual horn measurements. Use a matching metal horn with separate horn-retaining and adapter-mounting screws. Whole-vehicle printing and powered tests remain held, including clutch slip calibration and journal wear.
